## 10. FAQ snippets

**Q: Does this work on an iPhone or Safari?**
No. MediaPipe Tasks-Vision and Web Bluetooth need Chrome or Edge (desktop, or Chrome on Android). Please use a laptop, desktop, Chromebook, or Android tablet.

**Q: Do I need a micro:bit?**
No. The webcam, mesh, detections, and Speech all work without any hardware. The micro:bit is only for the optional Bluetooth bridge.

**Q: Does the camera feed leave my device?**
No. The video and the AI both run in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

**Q: Can my students use it offline?**
Yes. Once you have the ZIP, `index.html` runs entirely from your hard drive. The first load caches the MediaPipe model; after that, no internet is required.

**Q: Can I use it across my whole school?**
Yes — buy the Site License ($249) for up to 30 teachers at one campus, or the District License ($599) for multiple schools.
