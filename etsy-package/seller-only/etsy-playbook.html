Cut to the laptop. Chrome tab opens index.html, mesh snaps on. 11 signal bars (X · Y · distance · yaw · pitch · roll · smile · mouth · eyes · visibility) start ticking at 10 Hz.
00:10–00:25DEMO 1
🎙 SAY
"Ten built-in detections. Watch. Smile… laugh… wink left… wink right… tilt… look left."

Rapid-fire: kid smiles → SMILE badge lights. Laughs → LAUGH. Winks left → WINK LEFT. Tilts head → HEAD TILT. Looks left → LOOK LEFT. Each badge flashes green as the signal bar spikes.

Full description

🎭 Face Tracking — a browser-based face mesh lab for kids, built on Google MediaPipe FaceLandmarker. Open one HTML file, allow the webcam, and 468 landmarks snap onto your face in real time. 11 live signals stream at 10 Hz, 10 pre-built detections fire as the kid plays, and the computer talks back in 12 languages — all from Chrome, no install, no account, no server. WHAT YOU GET • 🎯 Real-time face mesh — 468 landmarks powered by Google MediaPipe FaceLandmarker (runs fully in the browser, TensorFlow-free) • 📡 11 live signals at 10 Hz — X, Y, distance, yaw, pitch, roll, mouth-open, left-eye, right-eye, smile, face-visibility • ✨ 10 built-in detections — Smile, Laugh, Wow, Mouth-open, Blink, Wink Left, Wink Right, Head Tilt, Look Left/Right, Look Up/Down • 🗣 Text-to-speech in 12 locales — en-US, en-GB, fr-FR, es-ES, de-DE, it-IT, pt-PT, pt-BR, nl-NL, ja-JP, ko-KR, zh-CN, with rate and pitch sliders • 🎨 Toggleable overlays — full mesh, contours (face oval, lips, eyes, brows, nose), raw landmark points • 📷 Front/back camera switch + mirror toggle • 🔗 Optional BBC micro:bit V2 bridge — streams a 19-digit signal packet over BLE UART / Nordic UART at 10 Hz • 💾 Ready-to-flash makecode.ts firmware included WHAT'S IN YOUR DOWNLOAD • index.html — the full app • makecode.ts — micro:bit firmware (flash once from MakeCode, optional) • SETUP.md — 5-minute quick-start • docs/ — illustrated guide, cheat sheet, FAQ, Etsy playbook • LICENSE — personal / classroom single-user license REQUIREMENTS • Any webcam (built-in laptop cam works) • Chrome or Edge on desktop (or Chrome on Android) • Optional: BBC micro:bit V2 for the BLE bridge • HTTPS or file:// — double-clicking the HTML works WHO IT'S FOR Teachers running STEM + AI workshops · home-schooling families · speech-therapy and special-needs practitioners using smile/blink feedback · robotics clubs wanting a face-controlled micro:bit · makers prototyping BLE face-input projects. PRIVACY FIRST Frames never leave your device. The model runs 100% in your browser — no analytics, no logins, no cloud, no uploads. Perfect for classrooms and kids.
