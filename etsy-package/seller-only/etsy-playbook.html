Full description

😊 Face Tracking Kids — See · Follow · Smile. A browser face-AI lab for kids, classrooms, and curious makers. Open one HTML file in Chrome or Edge and a live face mesh snaps onto your face. The AI runs on your device — no cloud, no uploads, no accounts. Smile, blink, open your mouth, turn your head — the app announces every event out loud. Optional: bridge a BBC micro:bit V2 over Web Bluetooth so your smile can light up the LED grid. WHAT YOU GET • 📷 LIVE WEBCAM — start, stop, switch between cameras, mirror on / off. • 🧠 FACE MESH — ~468-point landmark mesh powered by MediaPipe Tasks-Vision, running 100% on-device. • 🎛 THREE VIEWS — Mesh (full tessellation), Contours (eyes / lips / face outline), Points (raw dots). Any combination. • 😄 DETECTIONS — smile, kiss, open mouth, left wink, right wink, full blink, head turn, face present / absent. • 📊 NUMERICS — X, Y, distance, yaw, pitch, roll, mouth, left eye, right eye, smile, visibility — all live. • 🎪 DEMO MODE — the app calls each detection out loud as it happens. • 🔊 SPEECH — 13 voice languages (EN-US/GB, FR, ES, DE, IT, PT-PT/BR, NL, JA, KO, ZH) with adjustable rate and pitch. • 📡 OPTIONAL BLUETOOTH BRIDGE — pair a BBC micro:bit V2 and stream a 19-digit face packet (X/Y/Z/yaw/pitch/mouth/eyes/roll/smile/visibility) over Web Bluetooth UART. • 🧩 MAKECODE STARTER — drop makecode.ts into MakeCode, flash to V2, and your micro:bit reacts to smiles and blinks. • 🌐 RUNS LOCALLY — no server, no install, no account. Just open index.html. WHAT'S IN YOUR DOWNLOAD • index.html — the app • styles.css, logo.svg — the UI • makecode.ts — the matching micro:bit sketch (optional) • README.md + USERGUIDE.html — full walkthroughs • etsy-package/ — printable quick-start, control cheat sheet, A3 classroom poster, sticker sheet, lesson plan REQUIREMENTS • Chrome or Edge (Web Speech + Web Bluetooth — Safari & iOS are not supported) • Any webcam (built-in, USB, or phone) + good front lighting • Optional: BBC micro:bit V2 for the Bluetooth bridge only • Optional: headphones for the Speech output WHO IT'S FOR Teachers running AI / computer vision / STEM lessons · home-schooling families · kids 8–15 curious about how a phone unlocks with a face · makers building face-aware micro:bit projects. PRIVACY FIRST The camera feed and the face-landmark AI both run in your browser. No cloud AI, no accounts, no analytics. Video never leaves your device. Safe for school networks. LIFETIME UPDATES Every v1.x release shipped from this listing is yours forever. Re-download the latest ZIP anytime from your Etsy Purchases page. See · Follow · Smile 😊
