Sample Lesson: Can a Computer See Your Smile?

Ready-to-teach 45-minute example
Can a Computer See Your Smile?
45 minutes
Ages 8–15
AI / Computer Vision / Digital Literacy
Learning Objectives
1. Explain what a "face mesh" is and why it has many points.
2. Identify at least 4 events the app can detect (smile, open mouth, blink, head turn, face present/absent).
3. Describe how landmark positions become numbers (X, Y, yaw, pitch, roll).
4. Give one real-world use of face tracking and one privacy concern.

App features used in this lesson

  • Webcam + live mesh on-device AI, nothing uploaded
  • Mesh / Contours / Points three ways to visualise landmarks
  • Demo + Speech the app announces smiles, blinks, head turns
  • Numerics panel X, Y, yaw, pitch, mouth, smile, visibility
  • BLE Live Link optional micro:bit bridge over Bluetooth
