Face Tracking Kids

Browser face-AI lab for kids 8–15
v1.0.0 • Digital Download
Thank you for buying Face Tracking Kids! You just grabbed a zero-install, zero-account, browser-local face-tracking lab. The camera feed and the AI mesh both run on your machine — no cloud, no telemetry, no uploads. This page walks you through the first three things to do.

First 3 Things To Do

1
Open the app. Double-click index.html in Chrome or Edge. Everything loads locally.
2
Start the webcam. Click Start webcam and allow camera access. A live face mesh appears within a second.
3
Try Demo + Speech. Turn on Demo ON, then Start Speech. Smile, open your mouth, wink, turn your head — the app calls them out loud.

Requirements

✓Any laptop / tablet with a webcam (built-in or USB)
✓Chrome or Edge (desktop or Android)
✓Decent room lighting on faces — no backlight
✓Optional: BBC micro:bit V2 + MakeCode firmware from makecode.ts
✓Optional: headphones for the Speech output

Key Features

See: live webcam with face mesh, contour lines, or raw landmark points

Detect: smile • open mouth • wink / blink • head turn • face present/absent

Measure: X, Y, distance, yaw, pitch, roll, mouth, left eye, right eye, smile, visibility

Speak: 13 language voices, adjustable rate and pitch, "Test voice" button

Stream: optional Bluetooth Live Link — a 19-digit packet goes to a paired micro:bit V2

Privacy

100% Local — Zero Data Collection

The camera feed and the face-landmark AI run entirely in your browser. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking, no uploads. Video never leaves your device. Safe for school networks.
