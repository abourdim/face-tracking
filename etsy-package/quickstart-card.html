Face Tracking Kids

Browser Face-AI Lab for kids 8–15
No install • No account • 100% local • Webcam only
1
🌐

Open the App

Double-click index.html in Chrome or Edge. No server, no login. The camera feed & mesh run locally in the browser.

2
📷

Start the Webcam

Click Start webcam and allow camera access. Use Switch camera or Mirror ON/OFF if needed.

3
🧠

See the AI Mesh

A live face mesh draws on your face. Toggle Mesh, Contours, and Points to compare how the AI tracks features.

4
🎪

Try Demo Mode

Hit Demo ON and start Speech. Smile, open your mouth, wink, or turn your head — the app announces each one.

5
📡

Optional: Stream to a micro:bit

In the Live Link card, click Connect, pick your BBC micro:bit [xxxxx], then Start sending. Face position, yaw, pitch, mouth, eye, roll, smile and visibility stream as a 19-digit BLE UART packet. Use the paired MakeCode program to react to smiles, blinks, or head turns.
